What’s novel
Spectral consistency, measured on every result
The SR output is re-degraded with the training operator and compared with the 10 m input, pixel by pixel. A warnings note appears when the consistency projection is applied.
Per-pixel uncertainty map
Where the model is unsure (TTA disagreement or a learned σ), shown on a fixed scale, never min–max.
≤ 1 M parameters, INT8, CPU-only
A compact EDSR that runs on a laptop CPU with no GPU; size, backend and threads are in the header.

About the data

Real Sentinel-2 L2A 10 m inputs (B04, B03, B02, B08) paired with 2.5 m NAIP targets that the SEN2NAIPv2 authors co-registered and radiometrically harmonised to Sentinel-2 (crosssensor subset). Inputs are not synthetically degraded; matching per-band statistics between inputs and targets are expected from this harmonisation.

A training-time spectral loss was tested and rejected (it traded sharpness for consistency); consistency is measured on every result.
